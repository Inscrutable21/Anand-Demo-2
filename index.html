Hi, I'm Moritz

I am the head and heart behind Casa Momo and hosting is what I love most. I've enjoyed having guests for as long as I can remember.

What I care about is simple: that you walk in and feel looked after. My apartments aren't staged. They're warm, lively homes you can truly settle into, where comfort always comes first. I fill them with timeless pieces and special finds that carry a story of their own, and that give each apartment its own character.

I look after my guests personally and with real joy, before, during and after your stay.

My Philosophy

1

Design

Lived-in, not staged. Old-building charm, clean lines and carefully chosen details, a signature style that runs through each of my apartments.

Warmth

Every apartment has its own soul. Soft light, natural tones and a personal touch turn a place to stay into a feeling of home.
